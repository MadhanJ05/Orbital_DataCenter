How this twin works: positions come from real orbital mechanics (circular orbits at 650 km, sun-synchronous at 97.99°) and the real Sun for the date shown, and the fleet's load, background queue and power come from the Python twin's minute-by-minute model, run here from two days back (twin/twin3d_engine.js, checked by node twin/check_twin3d.js). Earth: NASA Blue Marble and Black Marble. Earth and orbits are to scale; satellites, sites and stations are drawn far larger than life. Our placement choices: each group's six planes fan out by 4 minutes of local time, and the 16 ground stations sit at existing optical, observatory and polar ground-station sites, linking above 20° of elevation. The model itself counts 12 planes at 650 km and 16 stations. Flat charts and every table: twin.html.
